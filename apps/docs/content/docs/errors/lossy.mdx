title: 'lossy'
description: 'A round-trip sample did not come back equal, so assertRoundTrip reported drift a lossy mapping shed.'
---

## What you'll see

A `BimorphError` with `code: "lossy"` and a message like
`round-trip drift: <input> → <output>`, thrown from
[`assertRoundTrip`](/docs/guides/testing/assert-round-trip) in your test suite — not
from a normal `decode` or `encode` call.

## Why it happens

`assertRoundTrip` ran a sample through `decode` then `encode` and the result wasn't
structurally equal to the input. The mapping shed something on the way — a rounding
step, a precision loss, a normalization — which is exactly what the
[`lossy`](/docs/guides/leaves/lossy) tier declares can happen but can't check at
creation time.

## How to fix

Decide whether the drift is the mapping working as intended:

- **If the loss is genuine and acceptable** — cents to a rounded dollar amount —
  the sample is proving the tier honest. Adjust the samples to the precision you
  actually promise, or accept that this pair doesn't round-trip exactly.
- **If the loss is a bug** — the `encode` doesn't invert the `decode` — fix the
  functions. If they _should_ round-trip exactly, the mapping is
  [`iso`](/docs/guides/leaves/iso), not `lossy`.
